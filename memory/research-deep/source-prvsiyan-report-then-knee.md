# RSNA Knee: read the report, then the knee
- ref: [prvsiyan/rsna-knee-read-the-report-then-the-knee](https://www.kaggle.com/code/prvsiyan/rsna-knee-read-the-report-then-the-knee) (current kernel v62; writeup lineage is V9 / Apache 2.0). Substantial overlap with [pilkwang/rsna-knee-baseline-v1](https://www.kaggle.com/code/pilkwang/rsna-knee-baseline-v1). Our public LB is 0.526.
- claimed_public: V9 is documented as official public AUC 0.847 (Kaggle submission `55332748`, `scriptVersionId=340808178`). An older comments crawl listed 0.829 on V7. Live v62 metadata does not attach a best-score field; kernel metadata names the revision `v10-proven-20-member-ensemble` and cites pilkwang baseline official source score 0.891. A guarded V9 reproduction scored 0.839.
- backbone / input: Reports are train-only. Imaging at fit/infer: six recovered series slots (plane × contrast) into twelve heads. V9 trains DINOv2 Small/Base (Kaggle model pins). Current v62 is a TPU v5e-8 (~24 min) stack: BiomedCLIP, DINOv2 Small+Base, EfficientNet-B3, plus mounted RadImageNet ResNet-50, MedicalNet ResNet-50, and other RSNA-2026 weights. Internet off.
- labels: `train.csv` has 4,407 studies, a `Report` on every row, and the twelve official columns filled on only 58 studies. `test.csv` has no `Report` column. So they do not fuse text at test. They turn every train report into twelve graded targets (trace / unqualified / marked, not a hard 0/1) plus a per-finding confidence that becomes a sample weight. Silence is not a confident negative.
  How the in-notebook reader works (nine languages, no language ID, one pooled lexicon):
  1. Fold case, diacritics, separators; NFKD so Greek MICRO SIGN `U+00B5` becomes real mu `U+03BC`.
  2. Unwrap hard-wrapped lines, then split clauses; glue `header:` onto the next line (`Fractures:` / `Aucune.` is one statement).
  3. Per clause: assert / negate / hedge. Directional negation (pre- vs post-negators, ~90-char window). Explicit normality = absence, unless a tear or high grade is in the same breath.
  4. Paired labels (ACL, MCL, both menisci) need anatomy + pathology; stem+side fallback for Turkish/Greek/Croatian morphology. Meniscus grade 3 = tear; grades 1-2 are not. Ligament grades run the other way.
  5. OA is mostly cartilage / osteophyte / joint-space language. Unlocalised cartilage can inherit to all three compartments at a discount (`oa_inherit`). Global cues (`gonarthrose`, tricompartmental) hit all three.
  6. Extra flags: `unwrap`, `directional_negation`, `graded_pathology`, `synovitis_backoff` (most reports never name synovitis).
  They also check the reader two ways that 58 gold rows can support: coverage / silence rate on all 4,407 (where the lexicon never fires), and agreement vs the 58 official labels (they treat gold-AUC as too noisy to pick lexicon tweaks). When a mounted LLM-read table is present (`pilkwang/rsna-knee-llm-labels`, and on v62 also `stevenleehans/…` and `lixin73/…`), that table supplies targets and the regex is the fallback.
- CV: Holdout AUC is computed only after binarising graded targets at their midpoint. That cut picks epochs/arms, never the submitted scores. Split must keep studies that share a verbatim report together. Combine arms by per-column rank mean, not probability mean (macro AUC only reads order). V9: five arms, 16 epochs, T4. v62: 20-member ensemble.
- inference: No reports on test. Drop the text branch. Decode the six slots, run the imaging arms, rank-mean the twelve columns, write `StudyInstanceUID` + the twelve header names (keep `Baker's`). Do not fill empty train labels with 0.
- copyable next step: Ship the in-notebook graded multilingual extractor (normalize → unwrap → clause → polarity → weights). Train a pure imaging model on those scores. At test, never look for `Report`. Prefer rank-mean if more than one arm exists. Compare extractor vs the 58 gold rows and the silence rate. That is the first lift vs our 0.526 constant-ish run.
- do not copy: The v62 20-member TPU zoo and private weight packs. Treating unmentioned findings as hard 0. Averaging raw probabilities. A text encoder at submit time. Training five 16-epoch DINOv2 arms on T4 as the first experiment. Blind-mixing three LLM label datasets without checking them against the 58 gold rows.
